Psychotropic Monitoring Guide

Antipsychotics and mood stabilisers. Select a medicine to see the required monitoring.

How to use Select a medicine to see its monitoring requirements. The list is not exhaustive, and extra monitoring depends on individual patient risk factors. Document all results, including refusals, in the notes, and for community patients also communicate them to the GP. Confirm against the current SHPU guidance before use. National recommendations are in NICE CG178 [1]. Sources are listed at the end.
